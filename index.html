The game

Toadstool

Toadstool is a super realistic psychology game about a toad, a lab, and a lot of mushrooms. A full storyline is coming in about six to nine months.

Early access is planned for the next month or two, with every mushroom in a small simulator. It is not a huge map, and it is not a demo. You get the whole lab and an outside area, NPCs to talk to, a library to look around, and orders you can place to have supplies delivered to the lab.

You do all of the lab work yourself: agar, culturing, setup, and running your own mycology lab to grow mushrooms.

Early access will be on Steam for about $14. Early supporters will get an early access badge later on.
